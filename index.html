📐 How Mini-Split BTU, Tonnage & Circuit Sizing Formulas Work

1. Zone Base BTU: Floor Area (L × W) × Insulation Base BTU/sq ft

2. Ceiling Height Multiplier: 1 + ((Ceiling Height - 8 ft) × 0.08) for ceilings above 8 ft.

3. Zone Adjusted BTU: (Zone Base BTU × Ceiling Multiplier × Climate Factor × Room Exposure) + Kitchen/Occupant Gain

4. Commercial Head Rounding: Each room is matched to standard ductless indoor wall cassette ratings (6,000, 9,000, 12,000, 15,000, 18,000, 24,000, 30,000, or 36,000 BTU/hr).

5. NEC 440 Electrical Circuit Estimate: Assumes inverter-driven SEER2 heat pump EER (~11.5 BTU/Watt at peak load) with a 1.25× continuous-load NEC safety multiplier for Minimum Circuit Ampacity (MCA).
