# ❄️ Mini-Split BTU & Tonnage Sizing Calculator ❄️

> Interactive single-zone and multi-zone ductless mini-split heat pump BTU, tonnage, indoor air handler, and NEC circuit breaker sizing calculator.

A fast, zero-dependency HVAC sizing utility built for homeowners, DIYers, and HVAC contractors to size single-zone or multi-zone ductless mini-split heat pumps by room.

---

## Features

- **Single-Zone & Multi-Zone Room Schedule:** Add unlimited rooms/zones or pick from common presets (Living Room, Master Bedroom, Kitchen, 2-Car Garage, Sunroom, Basement).
- **Building Envelope & Climate Multipliers:** Adjusts for ceiling height (`6ft` to `30ft`), insulation & window quality, regional design climate zones, sun exposure, kitchen heat gain, and occupant count.
- **Commercial Indoor Head Matching:** Automatically maps each room's thermal load to commercial wall unit sizes (`6k`, `9k`, `12k`, `15k`, `18k`, `24k`, `30k`, `36k BTU`).
- **Outdoor Condenser & NEC 440 Electrical Guide:** Recommends outdoor condenser tonnage plus estimated peak watts, Minimum Circuit Ampacity (MCA), breaker rating, and AWG copper wire gauge.
- **Shareable Calculation Links & Spec Export:** Copy a complete HVAC specification sheet or generate a shareable pre-filled URL.
